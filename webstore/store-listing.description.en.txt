Screen Curtain is a Chrome extension that covers the page with an overlay.
Use it to hide information you would rather not show, or to protect your privacy when you step away.

■ Key features

・Three themes (Light / Dark / Glass)
  Switch the look of the overlay to suit your taste.
  With the Glass theme, you can adjust the blur strength with a slider (1 to 20 px).

・Drag to move and resize
  Drag the overlay to move it anywhere you like.
  Eight handles on the corners and edges let you resize it freely.
  The overlay follows when you resize the window.

・Settings remembered automatically
  Position, size, theme and blur strength are saved automatically.
  The overlay appears in the same state next time.
  The popup's "Reset" button restores the position and size, and the saved blur strength, to their defaults.

■ How to use

1. Click the toolbar icon
2. Choose a theme (for Glass, you can also adjust the blur strength)
3. Press the "Show overlay" button

To close the overlay, click the × button at the top right or press the Escape key.

※ The extension cannot be used on internal browser pages such as chrome://. If the overlay cannot be shown, the popup displays the reason.

■ Contact

You can report problems or send questions from "Contact support" at the bottom of the popup. The first time, you enter a confirmation code sent to you by email.

■ Privacy

・Showing the overlay and saving settings are handled entirely on your device
・Only when you choose to submit the contact form, the email address, optional name, message and confirmation code you enter are sent to Kagayoi Support
・No data is collected or sent for advertising, analytics or telemetry
